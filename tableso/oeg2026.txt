===== Discarded New League Format  ‹§disc›

At the start of the season, a controversial new league format was announced, consisting
of three "Rounds":

====== Round-1  ‹§rd1›

A single round robin between all 16 clubs.

====== Round-2  ‹§rd2›

Top-8 of Round-1 play double round robin in Group-1
Bottom-8 of Round-1 play double round robin in Group-2

====== Round-3  ‹§rd3›

Top-6 of Group-1 of Round-2 play single round robin in Group-3

Bottom-2 of Group-1 and Top-2 of Group-2 of Round-2 play single round robin in Group-4

Bottom-6 of Group-2 of Round-2 play single round robin in Group-5

Clubs carry over records from Round-2 (but not from Round-1)
  (how this was supposed to work for Group-4 is obscure)

NB: hosting rights in Round-3 were to be reversed from fixtures in Round-1;
    two teams to be relegated (presumably both from Group-5 although that
    is not completely clear from the published regulations which only said
    that the "Final Table Standing shall be drawn from points and goals
    accumulated in Round-2 and Round-3"; these also stated that "The club
    that is ranked in 1st Position in the Final Table Standing shall be
    declared the 1st Division Champions for the UPL 2025/26 Season", but
    did not specify explicitly that that club is to come from Group-3, as
    would seem logical).

Out of protest against this convoluted and fairly nonsensical new format,
Vipers refused to play its fixtures in the first three rounds;
after that, on Oct 18, the FUFA Executive Committee suspended the new format.
