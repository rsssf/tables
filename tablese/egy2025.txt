[Mar 12]

Pyramids      0-0 Masry
Ceramica Cl   bye

NB: Ahly withdrew because they insisted on having foreign referees appointed
    for their match against Zamalek; according to the league regulations, any
    withdrawal from a match carried a forfeit loss and an additional deduction
    of three points; when this ruling was applied, Ahly decided to withdraw
    from the league (which would have implied automatic relegation); then the
    league was interrupted for nearly a month until the points deduction was
    revoked, to the understandable dissatisfaction of Pyramids and Zamalek
