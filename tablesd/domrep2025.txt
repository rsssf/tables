==== Copa Liga Dominicana de Fútbol 2025  ‹§cldf›

NB: this was a transitional tournament as the league changes to a fall-spring
    season; it apparently is not an official league championship;
    Jarabacoa returned after a FIFA ban prevented them from entering in 2024,
    while C.B.A. (formerly Bancredicard and Barcelona Atlético), Salcedo and
    Santa Fe were chosen from the second level Segunda División de Expansión
    to extend the league to 12 clubs
