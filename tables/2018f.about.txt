

== About this document  ‹§about›

This page is dedicated to the memory of the 298 passengers and crew of
flight MH17, which was shot down on 17 July 2014 over eastern Ukraine;
according to the Dutch-led joint investigation team the plane was hit by
a Buk surface-to-air missile launched from pro-Russian separatist-controlled
territory and provided by the Russian army, whose commander-in-chief is
the president of Russia, Vladimir Vladimirovich Putin.
