== Asian Nations Cup 2024



==== Qualifying Tournament

NB: all countries which reached the third stage of the ‹Asian section, see page 2022q.html#asi› of the
    2022 World Cup qualifying tournament are automatically qualified for the
    final tournament of the 2023 Asian Nations Cup: Syria, China, Australia,
    Iran, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Japan, United Arab Emirates, Vietnam,
    South Korea and Lebanon.

Playoff Round

NB: the four worst fifth placed teams (Indonesia, Cambodia, Chinese Taipei and Guam)
    of the second stage of the ‹Asian section, see page 2022q.html#asi› of the 2022 World Cup qualifying tournament
    enter, to determine two entrants for the group stage.

NB: winners qualified for group stage qualifying tournament.

Group Stage  ‹§grp›

NB: the three worst second placed teams (Kuwait, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan), the third
    placed teams (Philippines, Jordan, Bahrain, Palestine, India, Kyrgyzstan, Malaysia,
    Turkmenistan), the fourth placed teams (Maldives, Nepal, Hong Kong, Singapore,
    Afghanistan, Mongolia, Thailand, Sri Lanka) and the three best fifth placed teams
    (Yemen, Bangladesh, Myanmar) of the second stage of the ‹Asian section, see page 2022q.html#asi› of the 2022
    World Cup qualifying tournament enter, together with the two winners of the above
    playoff round.
