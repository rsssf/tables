===== Final Four  ‹§chfin4›

NB: between the top-2 of both the ‹Opening Top-6 Playoff, see §optop› and the ‹Closing Top-6 Playoff, see §cltop›

NB: if one club wins both tournaments, they are champions automatically and the
      two runners-up (if different) play off for second place overall;
    if two clubs each once finish first and once second, they play off for the
      championship and second place:
    if three clubs finish among the top-2 in both tournaments (and none wins both),
      the best third-placed team (on aggregate record over both tournaments) also
      enters the final four.
