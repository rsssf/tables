== Women's World Cup 2023 (Australia and New Zealand)





==== Qualifying

Australia and New Zealand (co-hosts) qualified automatically.

‹UEFA, see page ../tablese/eur-women-qual2023› (11): Denmark, England, France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Norway, Ireland, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland
‹AFC, see page ../tablesa/asia-women2022› (5): Japan, Vietnam, China, South Korea, Philippines
‹CAF, see page ../tablesa/afr-women2022› (4): Nigeria, Morocco, South Africa, Zambia
‹OFC, see page ../tableso/oc-women2022› (0):  none directly
‹CONMEBOL, see page ../tabless/sam-women2022› (3): Brazil, Argentina, Colombia
‹CONCACAF, see page wom-gold2022› (4): United States, Canada, Jamaica, Costa Rica
‹Inter-Confederation Playoffs, see page wwc2023icpo› (3): Panama, Portugal, Haiti

NB: the matches on 6 Aug were the only ones from the "New Zealand" half of the schedule
    (groups A, C, E, G and the knock-out matches between the winners and runners-up of
    those groups) to be played in Australia, implying the Netherlands and Sweden had to
    change countries twice between the group stage and the quarterfinals; the rationale
    behind this (if any) is unclear.
